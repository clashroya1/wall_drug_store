Discover Wall Drug

A South Dakota icon since 1931

More than a stop. A story you’ll take with you.

01

Wall Drug began as a small prairie pharmacy in 1931, when Ted and Dorothy Hustead chose the tiny town of Wall as the place to build their family’s future.

In the hot summer of 1936, Dorothy invited road-weary travelers to stop for free ice water. Hand-painted highway signs carried the promise across South Dakota, and a simple act of hospitality became an American roadside legend. The store has grown into a destination for food, Western history, shopping and family adventure—but the ice water is still free.
