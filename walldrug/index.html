The idea that changed everything

A hot day. A handmade sign. A promise travelers never forgot.

01

In the summer of 1936, Dorothy Hustead listened to the steady stream of cars crossing the hot prairie. She had an idea: invite those road-worn travelers in for a glass of ice water—free.

Ted and a local high school boy painted a string of signs along Route 16. Before Ted made it back to town, thirsty families were already waiting. That simple welcome turned a struggling pharmacy into a South Dakota legend.
